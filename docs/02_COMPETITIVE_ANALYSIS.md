## Your Competitive Moat

### What Celonis Can't Match

1. **Price Point**
   - They can't serve $299/mo customers profitably
   - Their sales model requires $60K+ deals

2. **Self-Service**
   - Celonis requires implementation consultants
   - You can enable self-onboarding in minutes

3. **Vertical Depth**
   - Celonis is horizontal platform
   - You can build deep industry expertise

4. **Open Foundation**
   - PM4Py base = transparency, extensibility
   - No vendor lock-in story

5. **Speed**
   - Celonis moves slowly (enterprise pace)
   - You can iterate weekly
